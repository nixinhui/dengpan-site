Company Overview

Who We Are

Founded in 2023 and based in Longyan, Fujian, China, Longyan Dengpan Trading is a licensed import & export trading company. We provide one-stop sourcing, supply and export services for industrial equipment and spare parts, serving clients and retrofit projects for coal-fired power plants and paper plants.

We are the supply channel our clients rely on. A trader sells once; a channel partner stays. We keep qualified manufacturers on call, learn your plant's exact equipment, and turn China's manufacturing base into a dependable, repeatable source — the right part matched to a drawing or a worn nameplate, supplied again and again.

Today we supply clients across the Philippines, Southeast Asia and Africa, spanning power generation, construction and processing industries — from single emergency spares to complete retrofit packages.
