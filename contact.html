What to Include in Your Enquiry

Most delays in spare-parts sourcing come from missing data, not from price. If you can provide the four points below, we can usually confirm availability and price in one exchange.

01

Exact Identification

Part number, model, or a clear photo of the nameplate and the worn part.

02

Quantity & Urgency

How many units, and whether it is for stock or an ongoing outage.

03

Technical Spec

Material, pressure class, size, voltage, rotation — whatever applies.

04

Delivery & Documents

Destination port, Incoterm, and certificates required for clearance.
